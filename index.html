About

A generalist by necessity, a specialist by curiosity.

I'm a game developer and technical artist who moves comfortably between gameplay logic and visual systems. Most of my work sits at the intersection of the two: building the tools, shaders and effects that let a gameplay idea actually feel good to play.

My day-to-day covers Blueprint and C# gameplay programming, Niagara and VFX work, shader and material authoring, and post-process tuning — along with the less visible part of the job: keeping projects readable, maintainable and performant as they grow.

I enjoy working on small, focused systems as much as full features: a wallride mechanic, a boot sequence, a retro post-process pack. I'm still building my experience, and I like projects where I can learn something new while delivering something solid.
